G2E 2025Showcase reel · feature wall

Shown at G2E, the industry’s largest trade show. The reel cuts together the in-game hero animations — entrances, idles, celebrations and win states; the wall art was printed at stand scale.

Heroes32 characters · one per club

Every club in the league gets its own hero. Each carries a distinct silhouette, props and palette while staying on-model with the rest of the roster.

The cabinetWhere it all ends up

The characters do not stop at a render. They ship inside a physical Aristocrat cabinet — dual portrait screens, a topper running the full roster, and the progressive meters climbing above the reels.

Everything on that machine, from the hero leaping out of the attract loop to the symbols on the reels, started as the same asset set on this page.
