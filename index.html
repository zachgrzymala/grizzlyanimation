Who's behind it

Hi, I'm Zach

It started with WALL·E — a small robot with no dialogue carrying a whole film on personality alone. That settled the argument for me in about ninety minutes.

I earned my BFA in Computer Animation at Ringling College and learned every station of the pipeline: modeling, texturing, rigging, animation, lighting, comp. Since 2023 I've been an Animator at Aristocrat Gaming, building original character content end to end. Along the way I sculpted holiday merchandise characters for the Walt Disney Creative Group and boarded a national TV spot at PSYOP.

What I care about is weight and intention — the pause before a character commits, the beat after they fail.
