How I got hereBio

It started with WALL·E. I was a kid in Bel Air, Maryland who had already cycled through basketball, dance and acting, and then I watched a small robot with no dialogue carry an entire film on personality alone. That settled the argument in about ninety minutes.

I studied art and animation through high school and found my way to Ringling College of Art and Design, which turned into less a school than a sanctuary for the work. I graduated with a BFA in Computer Animation having learned every station of the pipeline — modeling, texturing, rigging, animation, lighting and comp — and directed my thesis short, Rebound, start to finish.

Since 2023 I have been an Animator at Aristocrat Gaming in Las Vegas, building original character content end to end for titles that ship to casino floors worldwide. Before that I sculpted holiday merchandise characters for the Walt Disney Creative Group, boarded a national TV spot at PSYOP, and built a commercial spot at MAKE Visual Studios.

What I care about is weight and intention — the pause before a character commits, the beat after they fail. I work primarily in Maya, ZBrush and Substance Painter, and the thing I most want to do next is character performance on a feature crew.
